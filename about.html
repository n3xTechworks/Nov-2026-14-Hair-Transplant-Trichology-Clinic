Chronological Milestones

Our 15-Year Journey from Istanbul Clinic to Global Standard

A steadfast commitment to scientific innovation, transparent patient outcomes, and surgeon-directed care across fifteen years of clinical growth.

2011 · Foundation in Istanbul Levent

Dr. Elif Aydan founds HairCare Clinic with a two-room surgical suite, establishing our non-delegation philosophy and the strict rule of one surgery per day.

2011
2014

2014 · International Accreditation & Micro-FUE

Achieved ISO 9001 and JCI certification. Transitioned entirely to 0.75mm micro-motor extraction, reducing donor harvesting scar tissue by 65%.

2017 · Sapphire Gemstone Blade Integration

Pioneered custom V-shaped sapphire micro-blades in Turkey. Published peer-reviewed clinical findings demonstrating 40% faster crust shedding.

2017
2020

2020 · Choi Implanter Pen (DHI) Mastery

Integrated Direct Hair Implantation (DHI) pens for unshaven female restorations and high-density crown whorl reconstructions without recipient slits.

2023 · Cellular Trichology & Exosome Lab

Inaugurated our on-site cellular processing cleanroom, introducing autologous exosome protocols and digital epiluminescence follicle tracking.

2023
2025

2025 · 12,000+ Completed Transformations

Surpassed 12,000 documented international surgeries with verified 98.4% graft survival rate and patients traveling from 42 countries worldwide.

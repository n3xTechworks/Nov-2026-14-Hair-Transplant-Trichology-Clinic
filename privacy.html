Medical Data Protection

Patient Data Privacy and Photographic Confidentiality

At HairCare Clinic, we maintain strict medical confidentiality standards under international healthcare regulations, HIPAA guidelines, and GDPR mandates.

1. Collection of Health and Trichological Data

When you submit a consultation request, request a photographic hair density analysis, or visit our clinic in person, we collect medical information necessary to evaluate your suitability for surgical and regenerative trichology treatments.

  • Scalp photographic records and Norwood scale stage evaluations.
  • Relevant dermatological history and family predisposition to androgenetic alopecia.
  • Contact information including verified phone and email for procedure coordination.

2. Strict Photo Confidentiality Guarantee

Your pre-operative and post-operative scalp photographs remain strictly confidential. They are stored on encrypted medical servers accessible only by your board-certified surgeon and lead trichologist. We never publish patient photographs without explicit, written, opt-in consent.

3. No Commercial Data Sharing

HairCare Clinic does not sell, rent, lease, or monetize patient lists or clinical metrics to any third-party marketing brokers or commercial entities.

4. Right to Deletion and Portability

You retain full legal rights to request access to your trichological records, demand deletion of non-statutory pre-consultation photos, or revoke digital marketing consent at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@yourbrand.com.

The Cellular Science

The Three Biological Phases of Non-Surgical Retention

Our protocols intervene directly at the cellular level to prolong anagen growth cycles and protect follicles against systemic and androgenic degradation.

Phase 1
Arresting Miniaturization

Growth factors counteract 5-alpha reductase stress signals, halting the thinning of follicular papillae and reducing shed count.

Phase 2
Microvascular Reperfusion

Photobiomodulation and VEGF increase capillary density around each follicle, supplying vital amino acids and oxygen.

Phase 3
Terminal Shaft Calibre Gain

Exosome microRNA prompts dormant bulb stem cells to re-enter anagen, transitioning fine fuzz into strong, terminal strands.

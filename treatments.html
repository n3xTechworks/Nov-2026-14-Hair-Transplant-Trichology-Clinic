Technical Deep Dive

The Engineering Precision of Choi Implanter Pens

Traditional hair restoration requires two separate actions: cutting channels with blades, then pushing follicles into slits with forceps. DHI Choi pen technology merges both actions into one seamless movement.

Zero Pre-Made Recipient Incisions
The hollow needle pierces skin and places the graft simultaneously, eliminating tissue tearing and adjacent follicle damage.
3D Control Over Angle & Depth
Surgeons dial angle down to 10° for temporal peaks and calibrate needle depth to 2.8mm to match individual dermal thickness.
Unshaven & High-Density Feasibility
Allows seamless insertion between existing long hairs, ideal for crown thinning and women who cannot shave recipient zones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Your Procedure

Transparent medical answers from Dr. Elif Aydan regarding pain management, recovery timelines, graft survivability, and pricing.

Does hair transplant surgery hurt during or after the procedure?
No. We administer a specialized pressure-based local anesthesia system prior to micro-injections, rendering the entire donor and recipient area completely numb. Patients comfortably watch films, listen to podcasts, or rest during their session. Post-operative discomfort is mild and easily controlled with routine pain relievers for the first 48 hours.
How soon can I return to work and physical exercise?
Most sedentary desk workers resume remote or office duties within 2 to 4 days. Light walking is encouraged from Day 2. Strenuous weight training, cardiovascular workouts, swimming pools, and sauna sessions should be avoided for 14 days to protect newly anchored grafts.
What is the difference between Sapphire FUE and DHI?
In Sapphire FUE, micro-channels are opened using gemstone sapphire blades before manual graft insertion, making it ideal for large surface areas (3,000+ grafts). In DHI (Direct Hair Implantation), grafts are loaded into a specialized Choi implanter pen and inserted directly without pre-opening incisions, allowing superior density in hairlines without shaving existing hair.
Will my transplanted hair fall out over time?
Between weeks 3 and 8 post-op, transplanted shafts undergo temporary 'shock loss'—this is a normal biological shedding phase. The underlying follicle papillae remain alive and begin sprouting permanent, DHT-resistant terminal hair from month 3 onwards. Because donor follicles are extracted from the genetically resistant occipital safe zone, they retain their permanent characteristics for life.
Are international travel and accommodation packages included?
Yes. Our international comprehensive packages include 3 nights at our partner 5-star hotel in Levent, private VIP airport and clinic transfers, bilingual patient concierge, all surgical medications, and our specialized 1-year hair care kit.
